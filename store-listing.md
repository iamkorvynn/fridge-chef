# FridgeChef — Play Store listing

## Short description (80 chars max)

Snap your fridge, get real recipes you can cook with what you already have.

## Long description

FridgeChef answers the oldest kitchen question: what's for dinner, with what I already have?

**Photograph your fridge** — snap a photo and AI reads the ingredients for you (uses your own free Gemini API key, added in Settings).

**Or tick what you have** — pick from a checklist of common Indian and international ingredients, or type your own. Works fully offline, no key needed.

**Get recipes ranked by what you have** — 34 real recipes with accurate ingredients and steps: dal tadka, paneer butter masala, chole masala, masala dosa, chicken curry, pav bhaji, poha, khichdi and more. Each recipe shows its match percentage and exactly which ingredients are missing, so you know what to buy — or what you can cook right now.

**Cook with confidence** — full ingredients with quantities, numbered steps, prep and cook times, difficulty, veg / non-veg and max-time filters, and search by name.

**Save favourites** — your saved recipes live on your device. No account, no tracking.

Your ingredients and favourites never leave your phone. Only the photo you choose to analyse is sent to Gemini, using your own key.

## Feature bullets

- AI fridge photo detection (your own Gemini key, stored only on your device)
- Offline manual ingredient checklist, grouped by category
- 34 real bundled recipes, ranked by % of ingredients you have
- Missing-ingredient list on every recipe
- Veg / non-veg and max-time filters, plus search
- Full ingredients + numbered steps on every recipe
- Favourites saved locally in SQLite — no account needed
- No ads, no tracking
